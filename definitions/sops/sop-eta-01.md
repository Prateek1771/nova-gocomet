id: SOP-ETA-01
title: ETA slip on the ocean leg
exception_type: ETA_SLIP
---
## When this applies
The carrier's ETA at the port of discharge has moved later than the planned ETA by more than the tenant's threshold (24 h for most customers, 12 h for time-critical accounts). Small swings inside the threshold are normal carrier noise and need no action.

## Steps
1. Confirm the new ETA against the carrier's latest schedule and the vessel's AIS position before acting.
2. Check whether the slip pushes delivery past the customer's required delivery date or a booked trucking or rail slot.
3. If the delivery date is at risk, notify the consignee within 4 hours with the new ETA and the cause the carrier gave.
4. Re-plan on-carriage: move the trucking appointment and warn the warehouse of the new arrival window.

## Customer communication
State the original ETA, the new ETA and the reason in one sentence. Do not promise a recovery the carrier has not confirmed.
