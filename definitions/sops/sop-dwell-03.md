exception_type: DWELL
---
## When this applies
Dwell over 72 hours at SGSIN, LKCMB or MYPKG during declared congestion, affecting several containers on the same lane.

## Steps
1. Group the affected shipments and raise one consolidated case with the carrier.
2. Ask for priority loading for time-critical boxes and accept a later vessel for the rest.
3. Consider re-routing new bookings on the lane via a direct service until congestion clears.

## Customer communication
Send one consolidated update per customer listing every affected container, its position and revised ETA.
