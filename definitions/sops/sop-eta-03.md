---
id: SOP-ETA-03
title: ETA slip on time-critical or reefer cargo
exception_type: ETA_SLIP
---
## When this applies
Any ETA slip over 12 hours on a shipment flagged time-critical (production line supply, promotions, perishables) or carrying reefer containers.

## Steps
1. Treat as customer-impacting by default; do not auto-close.
2. For reefers, confirm with the carrier that the set point and power supply are maintained for the extra days.
3. Notify the consignee within 2 hours and the customer's account manager in parallel.
4. Pre-book priority discharge and customs pre-clearance at destination to win back time.

## Customer communication
Give the new ETA, confirm the cargo condition monitoring status, and name the recovery actions already taken.
