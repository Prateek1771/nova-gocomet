---
id: SOP-ETA-04
title: Minor ETA slip with no delivery impact
exception_type: ETA_SLIP
---
## When this applies
The ETA moved past the threshold, but the shipment still arrives before the customer's required delivery date with at least 48 hours of buffer, and no on-carriage booking is affected.

## Steps
1. Record the new ETA on the shipment and update the on-carriage plan.
2. No customer notification is required; close the exception with a note of the buffer remaining.
3. Re-open if a further slip consumes the buffer.

## Customer communication
None required. The customer portal shows the new ETA automatically.
