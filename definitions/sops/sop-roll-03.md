title: Peak-season rollover prevention
exception_type: ROLLOVER
---
## When this applies
Rollovers during peak season (August to October, pre-Chinese New Year) on high-demand lanes out of China and Vietnam.

## Steps
1. For time-critical cargo, move to premium or guaranteed-space products for the following weeks.
2. Split large bookings across two sailings to reduce the impact of a single roll.
3. Confirm space with the carrier 72 hours before cut-off for every booking on the lane.

## Customer communication
Advise the customer of the peak-season risk and the mitigation chosen for their next bookings.
