id: SOP-DWELL-02
title: Dwell and free-time exposure
exception_type: DWELL
---
## When this applies
Any dwell over 72 hours where the extended transit risks exceeding the free time agreed for destination demurrage or detention.

## Steps
1. Recalculate the free-time window at destination using the revised ETA.
2. Where the delay is the carrier's, request a free-time extension in writing before arrival.
3. Warn the customer's finance contact of potential demurrage and detention charges and the extension status.

## Customer communication
Quantify the possible charge per day and say what has been requested from the carrier.
