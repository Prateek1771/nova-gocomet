## When this applies
A Maersk container misses its connection at a Maersk hub (Tanjung Pelepas, Algeciras, Salalah or Singapore).

## Steps
1. Open a case through the Maersk customer portal using the container number and request the next-available re-plan.
2. Maersk re-plans within 24 hours; verify the new vessel in the portal before notifying.
3. If no re-plan appears within 24 hours, call the Maersk hub desk.

## Customer communication
Confirm the Maersk re-plan with vessel and ETA once it is visible in the portal.
