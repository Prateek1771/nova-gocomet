## When this applies
An MSC booking is rolled to a later sailing.

## Steps
1. Check the myMSC portal for the new vessel; MSC usually rolls to the next weekly sailing on the same service.
2. If the next sailing is more than 7 days later, ask the MSC booking desk for space on an alternative service.
3. Update the shipment with the new vessel once confirmed in myMSC.

## Customer communication
Confirm the new MSC vessel and ETA once visible in myMSC.
