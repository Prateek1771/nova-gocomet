---
id: SOP-DWELL-01
title: Long dwell at a transhipment port
exception_type: DWELL
---
## When this applies
A container has been discharged at a transhipment port and not loaded onto its connecting vessel for more than 72 hours.

## Steps
1. Ask the carrier for the confirmed connecting vessel and voyage, and the reason for the wait (congestion, vessel delay, space).
2. Check the impact on the final ETA and on the customer's delivery date.
3. If the box has no confirmed connection within 24 hours, escalate to the carrier's hub operations desk.
4. Notify the consignee within 4 hours when the final ETA moves past the delivery date.

## Customer communication
Explain where the container is, since when, and the confirmed next vessel. Give the revised ETA.
