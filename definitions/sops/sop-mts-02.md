---
id: SOP-MTS-02
title: Carrier escalation for repeated missed connections
exception_type: MISSED_TS
---
## When this applies
A missed transhipment on a lane where the same carrier missed a connection in the last 30 days.

## Steps
1. Log the incident against the carrier's scorecard.
2. Escalate to the carrier's account manager and request root cause and a service recovery plan.
3. Review the lane allocation with procurement if misses continue.

## Customer communication
As in SOP-MTS-01; also tell the customer the carrier has been escalated.
