---
id: SOP-ETA-02
title: Large ETA slip (over 48 hours)
exception_type: ETA_SLIP
---
## When this applies
The ETA has slipped by more than 48 hours, typically from a port omission, a blank sailing or extended congestion at a hub.

## Steps
1. Escalate to the carrier's customer service desk and request a written recovery plan.
2. Assess alternatives: transload at an earlier port, switch the remaining leg to another service, or air freight for critical lines.
3. Notify the consignee the same business day and offer the alternatives with cost and time impact.
4. Flag any detention or demurrage exposure at destination caused by the new arrival date.

## Customer communication
Lead with the impact on the delivery date, list the options with their trade-offs, and ask the customer to choose within 24 hours.
