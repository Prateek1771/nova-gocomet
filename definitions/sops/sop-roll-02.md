title: Rollover compensation and claims
exception_type: ROLLOVER
---
## When this applies
Cargo was rolled although it was delivered before the cut-off with documents complete, under a contract that guarantees space.

## Steps
1. Collect proof: gate-in time, VGM submission and the booking confirmation.
2. File a service failure claim with the carrier under the contract's space guarantee clause.
3. Inform the customer that a claim is open; do not promise compensation.

## Customer communication
A factual update on the new vessel plus a note that we are pursuing the carrier under the contract.
