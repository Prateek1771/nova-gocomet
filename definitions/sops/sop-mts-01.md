---
id: SOP-MTS-01
title: Missed transhipment connection
exception_type: MISSED_TS
---
## When this applies
The planned connecting vessel has departed the transhipment port without the container on board.

## Steps
1. Get the carrier's confirmation of the next available vessel and its ETA at the port of discharge.
2. Request priority loading on that vessel; if the next sailing is more than 3 days away, ask for an alternative service.
3. Recompute the final ETA and re-plan on-carriage.
4. Notify the consignee within 4 hours with the new vessel name and ETA.

## Customer communication
Say that the container missed its connection at the hub, name the new vessel and the new ETA, and confirm priority loading was requested.
