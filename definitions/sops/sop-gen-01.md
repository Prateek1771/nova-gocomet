title: Exception triage and closure
exception_type: GENERAL

## When this applies
Every shipment exception raised by monitoring, before a type-specific SOP is applied.

## Steps
1. Verify the facts behind the exception (the metric query and its rows) before acting on it.
2. Decide whether it is actionable: an exception with no impact on delivery or cost can be closed with a note.
3. Apply the SOP for the exception type and record which SOP was followed.
4. Close the exception only after the customer has been informed or the decision not to inform is recorded.

## Customer communication
One clear message per exception, sent by the ops lead who owns the account.
