title: Booking rollover
exception_type: ROLLOVER
---
## When this applies
The carrier moved the booking to a later vessel than the one confirmed (rolled cargo), at origin or at a transhipment port.

## Steps
1. Re-book on the next sailing and confirm the new vessel, voyage and cut-off.
2. Notify the consignee within 4 hours with the new vessel and ETA.
3. Check the free-time impact at destination and the validity of any booked trucking.
4. Record the rollover against the carrier for the quarterly service review.

## Customer communication
Name the original and new vessel, the new ETA and whether the document cut-off changes.
